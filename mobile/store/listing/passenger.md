Angren Taxi is a taxi and delivery app built for the city of Angren. Enter your destination, see the fare upfront, and a driver arrives in minutes.

📦 CARGO & PARCELS

🍽 FOOD & MARKET
• Order from restaurants and shops in Angren
• Follow your order status

🛡 SAFETY
• SOS button during every ride

• Every driver's documents are verified

⭐ CONVENIENCE
• Saved places — home, work
• Rate your driver after the ride
• Lost & found section
• Available in Uzbek and Russian
